Our Story

Why settle for another plain latte or supermarket cake? Bittersweet brings something different. We’re a handcrafted coffee and dessert catering company led by a barista who loves creating unforgettable moments. Favorites like our Donut Coffee and Crispy Chocolate Fruit Tart always win hearts.

From weddings and corporate events to birthdays and holiday gatherings, we’ve done it all with warmth and charm. We use only the finest beans, design desserts that feel special, and tailor every package to your occasion.

Our mission is simple: to elevate your event with coffee and desserts that connect people, spark joy, and leave lasting memories.
